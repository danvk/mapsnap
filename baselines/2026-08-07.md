## What moved, and why

**#263 is a superset win, not just a repair.** The 08-05 arm was legacy-eraser-
only, 08-06 was precise-only; 08-07 picks the best of raw/precise/legacy per
box. Three volumes beat *both* single-eraser worlds:

- **nashville 65.7** (+7.7 over baseline, +24.3 over 08-06). Disasters 3 pages
  -> 1: p4 410->26 ft, p28 1304 ft->unfit, p16 32->8.6 ft. Pages the legacy
  eraser had been quietly hurting now take the raw or precise read — wins
  unreachable from either single arm.
- **brooklyn 87.9** (+2.2), 54 placed, zero disasters.
- **miami 71.6** (+3.5).

**champaign 92.5 (−5.3)** — the #263 target page recovered fully (p20__1 26.8
-> 5.2 ft, S NEIL back to 0.476). The residual is a *different* page: `W. HILL`
rose 0.229 -> 0.431 (arbitration picking the raw read where 08-05 was forced
to use the legacy-erased one), clearing the size floor and joining p21__1's fit
as a 9th inlier. A top-edge label acts as a rotation lever (+1.20° -> +2.06°),
and champaign's split panels are cross-matched, so the truth panel p21__2 is
scored against p21__1's fit at extrapolation distance: 19.4 -> 25.9 ft, just
over the line. Its 0.2475 km² is exactly the 5.3-point gap. Borderline, and
amplified by champaign's 33-page / 4.66 km² denominator.

**los_angeles 79.2 (−1.8)** — reproduced exactly from 08-06, so it is a stable
property of the merged fixes, not noise: p1499K__1 (0.24 km², 13.9 ft ->
unfit), the 6 km multi-region key-map page, partly offset by p1499N__3 and
p1404__2 improving.

**Everything else reproduced 08-06 to the decimal**, which is the intended
signature: #263 only touches boxes an eraser fires on.

## Known next lever (measured, not yet implemented)

Truth within-rung scale spread is far tighter than the pipeline's bands: p50
0.67%, p95 3.98%, **p99 7.59%**, with only 0.6% of pages beyond 10%. Against
that, `SAME_SCALE_BAND = (0.75, 1.25)` is ±25% — 3.3x the p99 — and the bands
are mutually inconsistent (HALF ±20%, DOUBLE ±10%).

No page scoring <=25 ft anywhere in the corpus has a scale error >=8% (worst:
fargo p69__3 at 7.4%). A gate at 8% catches 51 pages — 19 disasters, 32
mid-tier, **0 good** — worth **+0.79 corpus points**, concentrated exactly
where the corpus is weakest: **nashville +8.13, fargo +4.06** (the latter is
p59__2, a 22.2% misscale currently counting as a 239 ft disaster).

Risk: at runtime the comparison is against the volume rung, not truth, so
genuinely odd-scale sheets are exposed. There are 12 corpus-wide, of which
only **one** is currently fit well enough to lose (columbus p202__1, 10.4%
off-rung, 17.1 ft). Proposed: tighten all three bands to a consistent ±10%.
