## Run notes

- Wall clock 14:15 → 21:12 (~7 h): lane A serial rerun `--no-fit` smallest-first,
  lane B fits 2-up. Fresh OCR for all ~2,100 pages+panels was the long pole.
- **Aborted first start**: OCR initially ran under `--resume` semantics; caught
  after one volume. Keymap regeneration invalidates keymap-restricted reads, so
  all `streets.json` were deleted and OCR rerun fresh. baseline.md updated.
- **Richmond crashed twice in IIIF mask generation** (GEOS side-location
  conflict, then a non-noded intersection): latent fragility in
  `_fill_coverage_gaps` exposed by this run's mask shapes. Fixed on main
  (`5845f2b`, `04427eb` — `safe_unary_union` + `safe_overlay` throughout the
  gap-assignment loop, write-path only); richmond refit cleanly after.
  Volumes fitted before/after the patch differ only in that write path.
- Pre-flight removed 38 stale `p*.contradiction.json` (doc step still required;
  fit does not clear these). The doc's stale-`candidates.jsonl` warning is
  obsolete — #349 clears snap/street-solve caches at fit start.
