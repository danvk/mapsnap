## Run notes

- Wall clock 15:42 → 22:27 (~6.75 h) for the 18-volume lanes, then 22:28 → 23:15
  for the Schenectady and Los Angeles redo. Lane A serial `rerun --no-fit`
  smallest-first, lane B fitting each volume as released.
- Pre-flight per volume: re-split; delete raw/ panel stragglers with no 25% image;
  delete split sheets' parent and panel `boxes.json`; delete `p*.streets.json`
  and `p*.contradiction.json`.
- **Aborted first start**: the lane list contained `data/schenectady_ab361`, a
  #361 experiment copy of Schenectady with its own mapsnap.json and truth. It was
  deleted and lane A restarted from grand_rapids. Rule: no experiment directories
  under `data/` that look like volumes.
- **Trap 1 — the lanes ran one commit behind origin/main.** #394 was merged on
  GitHub at 15:28 EDT but local main was never pulled; 17 manifests record
  14ecedc (#391) and craft took the old path ("0 derived" on all but four
  volumes, where a parent's boxes happened to exist). Measured effect is within
  noise — Richmond derived 86.3 vs detected 86.5, Chicago identical at 79.1 — so
  those 17 volumes stand as run. Schenectady and Los Angeles (redone) and
  Richmond carry the derivation path. Recorded as pre-flight item 0 in
  docs/baseline.md.
- **Trap 2 — a newly split key-map sheet leaves its parent's chain behind.** The
  corner-box detector (#389) cut Schenectady's p0 (its KEY legend) and Los
  Angeles's pa (the Vol-13 box) for the first time; the chain re-ran on p0__1 and
  pa__2, but the parents' `raw/*.keymap.json`, `.georef.json` and
  `.regions.panels.json` stayed, and both the locator and the adjacency repair
  glob them ("Using key map(s): p0, p0__1"). Every page's region was counted
  twice, doubling the summed area behind the region scale prior and shifting the
  volume median by √2; the two tiny inset panels of split sheets (p60__1 at
  538×669 px, p72__2 at 480×424) crossed into the half-scale band and were
  snapped to the 0.5× rung (24 → 459 ft, 8 → 218 ft; the fit log says "key-map
  region is 2.30x the volume's typical area ratio"). Fixed on this branch:
  `records.split_parent` — both consumers skip a sheet with a sibling
  `<stem>.panels.json`. Both volumes were redone on the fixed code (with #394's
  derivation, since their panels' boxes were cleared) and their first fits kept
  as `2026-09-03-dupkeymap` (Schenectady 77.8, Los Angeles 90.1).
- The IIIF builder's coverage-retention report used bare `unary_union`; a
  side-location conflict under the oracle's synthetic poses (Philadelphia) killed
  a build after every mask was computed. It now uses `safe_unary_union` like the
  gap-assignment loop; verified on the failing case.

## Oracle of eight

From each volume's archived `osm_snap/candidates.jsonl` (one record per page that
reached snap, up to eight ranked candidates, each with a 7×7-grid rmse against
truth). The oracle materializes its pick as corner-GCP sidecars (mask-free build)
and is graded by the real `mapsnap compare`, so the scores below are the
production metric.

Of 1,742 snap pages with truth, 1,375 (78.9%) hold a ≤25 ft candidate in their
top 8; 1,363 (78.2%) are already published ≤25 ft. The first good candidate is
rank 1 in 1,306 cases, rank 2 in 37, rank 3–7 in 32.

| | mean score, 20 volumes |
|---|---:|
| baseline | 80.4 |
| oracle, best of {incumbent, top 8} | **83.8** |
| oracle, best of the top 8 only | 81.3 |

The gap between the two oracles is 60 pages published ≤25 ft (from RANSAC and
the other channels) whose top 8 holds nothing good — 31 of them have a disaster
as the best candidate — which is what the gates are protecting.

Rescuable pages (published not ≤25, a good candidate exists): 72 (4.1%) — 38
unplaced, 30 mid-band, 4 disasters. For 43 of them (60%) the good candidate was
ranked first and a gate blocked it: 22 rescue-mode abstains on the select or
margin bar, 27 challenge/keep verdicts (the candidate agrees with a 27–66 ft
incumbent within 100 ft, drops to the refine path, and fails its verification
margin or name parity), 6 refine-accepted poses demoted downstream (#270's
ordering class). For the other 29 (40%) an alias out-ranked it. The oracle also
demotes 15 published disasters (11 to mid, 4 to good).
