## What moved

**The wins track the recognizer.** fargo +13.4 reproduces its held-out A/B
prediction (+11.5) almost exactly, and it was a holdout volume — the model had
never seen it. grand_rapids +8.5, columbia +5.7 and champaign +5.5 (now 98.0%,
zero disasters) are volumes the recognizer *did* train on, which is the
expected asymmetry: the holdout number is the pessimistic bound.

**The losses need attribution before they are believed.** Three of the five
are volumes where more than one change could bite:

- **chicago −4.4** is the one to open first. It was the designated risk volume
  for #281: seven of its inliers were flagged as P(road) collateral, and the
  user's manual review confirmed four as genuine street labels (p44N KINZIE —
  one of #116's own cautionary examples — p57W W ERIE, p61W W GRAND AV, p61W
  JEFFERSON). The A/B measured chicago flat at 79.5 with only p61W lost, so
  a −4.4 here is larger than that experiment predicted and is not yet
  explained.
- **washington_dc −3.6** placed *more* pages (119 → 121) and scored worse:
  placing more, worse. DC was not among the four volumes the P(road)
  threshold was calibrated on.
- **philadelphia −1.8** got its first-ever splits (0 → 2 panels) in this run,
  so its number is not comparable page-for-page with 08-07.
- **nashville −1.0** is the volume whose −0.9 in the #265 A/B was traced to
  p4 (#277, now fixed but the correct pose is absent from its candidate roll)
  and p47 (#281, which *did* recover it to 28.9 ft in the A/B). Worth
  re-checking those two pages specifically.

## Incidents

- **A fit lane fitted stale reads.** Mid-run I branched for a docs PR, which
  reverted the working tree to a main that lacked `--no-fit`; every lane-A
  call then failed instantly, and the lane script marked volumes ready
  regardless of exit code, so six volumes were fitted over `streets.json` from
  earlier runs. Caught within ~20 minutes, outputs purged, lanes restarted at
  23:09. **Fix: a marker file must mean the step succeeded, not that it ran** —
  lane A now gates the release on `rc=0`.
- **new_orleans_la_1896_vol_2 fit exited 245** during snap (no traceback in the
  log; OCR was clean). Re-running `mapsnap fit` on the same reads succeeded and
  produced 83.3%. Worth watching for a repeat.
